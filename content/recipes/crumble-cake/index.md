This has become one of my favorite desserts. It's not the most straightforward, but is equally not tremendously difficult either. Despite now serving it all the time to the same people, no has complained, which feels like a great endorsement. It's another recipe from the Ottolenghi team that I've tinkered with enough to commit some notes.

This works really well with other fruit as well. In the summer, I've swapped in peaches for the apples and raspberries for the blackberries.

My more North American-minded friends always request ice cream. I'm a fan of serving with a dollop of crème fraîche.

### Ingredients

#### Crumble topping

* 75 g unsalted butter, cold, cut into small cubes
* 90 g all-purpose flour
* 80 g raw sugar
* 1 teaspoon ground ginger
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 60 g flaked almonds
* Pinch salt

#### Cake

* 4 granny smith apples (ca. 350 g), peeled, cored, and thinly sliced
* 35 g fresh ginger, grated
* 1 lemon, zested and juiced
* 180 g unsalted butter, softened (ca. 20 °C)
* 130 g sugar
* 3 eggs
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 180 g all-purpose flour
* 1.5 teaspoons baking powder
* Pinch salt
* 300 g blackberries
* Unsalted butter

### Method

#### Crumble topping

In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, add all the ingredients. Then mix on a low speed until the mixture is sandy and homogeneous.

#### Cake

In a large bowl, toss together the grated ginger with the lemon juice, lemon zest, and apple slices. Leave those to macerate as the rest of the cake is prepared.

In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with its paddle attachment, add the butter and sugar, and cream them together. The mixture should be pale white and airy.

Then add the eggs and vanilla, and mix until just combined.

Sift in the flour, baking powder, and salt. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients by hand until just combined. Then fold all but a handful of the blackberries and macerated apples.

Preheat an oven to 170 °C (338 °F) with fan (convection) if available.

Grease a large baking dish with butter. Then pour in the cake mix and spread it into a roughly even layer. Arrange the remaining apples and blackberries in the top of the cake. Then add the crumble topping, taking care to leave some of the fruit visible through the crumble topping.

Bake the cake in the preheated oven until the top is golden and a cake tester comes out clean, about 45 minutes.
