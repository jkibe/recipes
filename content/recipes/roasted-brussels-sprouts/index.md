This is easily my favorite way to do brussels sprouts. The roasting concentrates and adds flavor, and the dressing gives it a great pop.

### Ingredients

#### For the sauce

* 60 g mirin
* 45 g light maple syrup
* 30 g white miso
* 15 g rice wine vinegar
* 15 g toasted sesame oil
* 10 g shoyu
* Salt

#### For the sprouts

* 700 g brussels sprouts, halved
* Ca. 30 g sunflower (or other neutral) oil
* Salt
* Cilantro, roughly chopped
* Toasted sesame seeds

### Method

#### For the sauce

Combine all the ingredients in a small saucepan, and whisk them together. Place the pan over medium heat and bring the contents to a light simmer. Cook the sauce until it's slightly reduced and thickened, 5 to 10 minutes. 

Season to taste with salt.

#### For the sprouts

Preheat an oven to 200 °C (400 °F) with fan (convection) if available.

In a large bowl, toss the brussels sprouts together with the oil and salt to taste. Transfer them to a large baking tray.

Transfer the tray to an oven and roast the sprouts until they're nicely browned and charred in places, 20 to 30 minutes.

Remove the roasted brussels sprouts from the oven. Immediately pour over the prepared sauce, and toss the brussels sprouts to evenly coat them with the sauce.

Sprinkle over toasted sesame seeds and cilantro.

Serve immediately.
